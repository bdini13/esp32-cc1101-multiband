## What the evidence means

- ERC/DRC reports describe configured checks at a recorded revision. They do
  not certify radio behavior, safety or manufacturability.
- Scripted checks cover selected properties, not every possible failure mode.
- KiCad renders/exports show saved design files, not working hardware, and
  may omit components with missing 3D models.
- Manufacturer reference images are attributed, not AI-created project assets.
- Partial routing is experimental and not approved for ordering.
- No physical unit or measured four-band result exists at the A2 checkpoint.

power/thermal budgets, USB/RF layout, source licenses and compliance. Record
